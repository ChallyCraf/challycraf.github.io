Your daily pooja, wherever you are

Place a photo of your favourite god, your kula deivam, or a beloved family member in a beautiful frame. Light the lamp, offer flowers and fruits, and keep the light burning day and night.

    A brass oil lamp that keeps burning in real time, even when the app is closed.
  • Fresh garlands
    Rose, jasmine and arali garlands for the frame.
  • Candles
    Or light candles, in the colours you choose.
  • Live wallpaper
    Your altar on your lock screen and home screen.
  • Unquenched lamp
    Count how long your light has burned without going out.
  • Private
    No sign-in. Your altar and photo stay on your phone.
